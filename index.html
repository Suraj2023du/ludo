How to Play

  1. Each player has 4 tokens parked in their base.
  2. Tap the dice on your turn. A token can only leave the base on a 6, landing on your coloured start square.
  3. Roll a 6 and you roll again. But three 6s in a row and you lose the whole turn.
  4. Move clockwise around the 52-square track. Tap a glowing token to move it.
  5. Land exactly on an opponent on a normal square and it is captured - back to base it goes, and you get another turn.
  6. The 8 safe squares (4 coloured start squares + 4 stars) are protected. Nobody can be captured there.
  7. Two or more of your own tokens may share a square. They are drawn side by side.
  8. After a full lap you turn into your private home column. Opponents can never enter it.
  9. You need the exact roll to reach the centre. Overshoot and that token cannot move.
  10. Bringing a token home does not give you an extra turn.
  11. First player to bring all 4 tokens home wins. Play continues so everyone else gets a rank.
  12. No legal move? Your turn passes automatically.
